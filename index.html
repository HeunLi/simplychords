Three steps to your first chord

Nothing to set up and nothing to sign in to.

  1. Open a song

    Any video on YouTube, including Shorts and YouTube Music.

  2. Click once and press play

    Browsers only let a page hear sound after you've clicked it, so give the page one click.

  3. Play along

    The chord to play now, its finger diagram, and the next chord with a countdown appear on the video.

Made for playing, not reading

Everything is built around keeping your hands on the guitar.

Analyze this song

Every chord shows up before it happens

Click Analyze this song and Simply Chords maps the whole song ahead of time. From then on each chord appears before the change, so you're never late.

Analyzed songs are remembered. Haven't analyzed one? Once the progression repeats, the next chord is predicted for you.

Your guitar, your way

Capo picked for you, easier shapes

Auto capo chooses one fret for the whole song that gives the easiest open chords. Tricky barre chords like F and Bm are swapped for shapes you can actually play.

Move the chord box, resize it, or set your own capo from the menu.

While you wait

Letter Fleet

Press G while a song is being analyzed and the letters on the page lift off and become a fleet of ships. Your ship is your profile picture.

Entirely optional, and it closes itself when your chords are ready.

Simple chords

Major and minor chords only. 7ths and power chords become the easy chord that fits the song's key.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no ads and no paid version.

Does it record or upload my audio?

No. The sound of the video is analyzed in your browser as it plays and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Which sites does it work on?

YouTube and YouTube Music, in Chrome and other Chromium browsers such as Edge.

How accurate is it?

Very good on clear guitar songs, and an analyzed song is more accurate than live listening. Busy mixes with loud vocals or heavy effects are the hardest. Inversions and slash chords (like G/B) aren't shown.

What guitar does it expect?

A 6-string guitar in standard tuning. Shapes are chosen to be comfortable on an acoustic, with a capo where it helps.

Is Simply Chords made by YouTube?

No. Simply Chords is an independent extension that works on YouTube. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
